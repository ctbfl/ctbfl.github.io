The preview, however, commits in pixels rather than in directly usable poses. A key insight we establish is that tidiness is a joint function of object-object spatial relationships and each object's individual placement and pose: registering objects independently recovers only the latter, and the uncoupled errors leave the depicted rows and alignments ragged. This insight motivates our core innovation: a dual-channel parsing of the preview. The VLM channel compiles the depicted relational structure into an arrangement program with open metric parameters; the perception channel fills them by registering reconstructed meshes against the preview, supplying the spacings, offsets, and off-axis orientations language cannot state precisely.
